# Behavioral Interview Preparation

## Question 1: How have you taken initiative on a project without being asked?

**Situation:** I wanted to explore LangGraph's capabilities and build something tangible.

**Task:** Learn LangGraph independently and ship a functioning Travel Assistant at a 48-hour hackathon.

**Action:**
* Researched LangGraph evenings—read docs, sketched workflow nodes
* Scouted a 48-hr hackathon and recruited 1 UI/UX designer + 2 backend engineers
* Ran a 5-minute kickoff: set goals, divided work (I'd wire LangGraph nodes; teammates handled APIs; designer polished UI)

**Result:**
* Delivered a fully working "Travel Assistant" demo that generated multi-leg itineraries from a single prompt
* Project featured on my résumé as evidence of proactive initiative and cross-functional leadership

## Question 2: Describe a situation where you collaborated with a cross-functional team

**Situation:** Led teams in 12× 24–48 hr hackathons, where rapid coverage of all technical areas is critical.

**Task:** Assemble and coordinate a cross-functional team (UX/UI, front-end, back-end, API) to ship an MVP by the final demo.

**Action:**
* Formed teams with friends specializing in:
  - 1 front-end engineer, 2 back-end engineers, 1 API specialist
* Aligned roles to each person's strengths and growth goals
* Filled any skill gaps myself—helping on UI, server logic, or API design as needed

**Result:**
* Delivered a polished demo every time
* Won one of the hackathons
* Earned judge praise for seamless UI/UX flow and rock-solid backend integration

## Question 3: Tell me about a time you faced a difficult challenge at work. How did you handle it?

**Situation:** AI quiz generator lacked enough clean data for some sub-topics, and DETR mis-parsed fragments.

**Task:** Enable quiz creation for every sub-topic despite sparse or noisy input.

**Action:**
* Built a summarizer to clean and augment raw snippets
* Added a fallback where an LLM first generated extra sub-topic content, then merged it
* Refined prompts with examples and metadata filters to focus on relevant facts

**Result:**
* Achieved 100% sub-topic quiz coverage
* Received solid praise from the product team for reliability and accuracy

## Additional Questions to Prepare For:

### Leadership & Initiative
- Tell me about a time when you received critical feedback. How did you handle it?
- Give an example of a time when you took initiative to solve a problem.
- Tell me about a time you spotted an opportunity and drove it from idea → prototype.
- Give an example of when you pared away complexity to ship faster.
- When did you challenge the status quo on your team or in your codebase?

### Collaboration & Communication
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker. How did you resolve the conflict?
- Tell me about a time you made yourself vulnerable to feedback and improved as a result.
- How do you solicit dissenting opinions when you're confident in your own design?
- Describe a situation where you admitted 'I don't know'—what happened next?
- Tell me about a time you leveraged someone's unique background to improve a project.
- How have you ensured every voice was heard on a culturally diverse team?
- Describe when you assumed good intent to resolve a misunderstanding.
- Give an example of celebrating differing perspectives to drive innovation.

### Adaptability & Problem-Solving
- Describe a situation where you had to adapt to a sudden change at work. How did you manage it?
- Describe a time you pivoted your approach mid-project—what did you learn?
- Tell me about a time you had to meet a tight deadline. How did you handle it?

### Work Style & Growth
- How do you prioritize your tasks when working on multiple projects?
- How do you stay up-to-date with industry trends and technologies?
- Do you work well in fast-paced environments?

### Company-Specific
- Why do you want to work at TikTok, and how do you see yourself contributing to our team?
